## 📧 How OTP Registration Works

### Step 1: Register as Admin
1. Go to **Register** page
2. Fill in your details
3. Select **"Admin"** from role dropdown
4. Submit the form

### Step 2: Receive OTP
- OTP will be sent to your email
- **OR** check backend console for OTP (development mode)

### Step 3: Verify OTP
- You'll be redirected to OTP verification page
- Enter the 6-digit OTP
- Click "Verify OTP"
- Account will be created and you'll be logged in automatically
